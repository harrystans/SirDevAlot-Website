**Placement:** Staffordshire Games Institute 1UP Summer Placement

**Project Overview:** During a competitive 6-week industry-simulated placement, I served as a Gameplay Programmer and Designer for DiscGO, a high-velocity arena game. I focused on creating a high-fidelity "feel" for the core mechanics and building scalable systems for environmental hazards.
<p><iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/9eClqfMqBQE" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe></p>

## Technical Highlights

**Input Architecture:** Engineered a comprehensive controller input system, ensuring frame-perfect responsiveness and precise behavioral mapping to meet commercial standards for competitive gameplay.

**Deterministic Physics Debugging:** Diagnosed and resolved complex physics-based anomalies within the sports game, stablising projectile trajectories and collision logic to ensure a consistent player experience.

**UI Systems Refactoring and Navigation:** Led a complete overhaul of the legacy UI navigation framework. Replaced prototype-level code with a scalable, controller-first architecture designed for seamless menu traversal and reliability.

**Production-Ready Settings and UI Functionality:** Architected and implemented the full suite of UI graphics and settings menus from the ground up, transforming non-functional prototype placeholders into a fully operational, ship-ready system.
