**Award:** Staffordshire Games Institute 1UP Summer Placement

DiscGO is a fast-paced arena game developed during the Staffordshire Games Institute’s 1UP summer placement scheme. I worked as a gameplay designer and programmer for 6 weeks (18 hours a week), contributing to a variety of core systems and features:

<p><iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/JDvtloIgJmc" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe></p>

## Key Contributions

- **Disc Controller:** Engineered responsive disc movement and control mechanics for competitive gameplay.
- **Arena Events:** Designed and implemented dynamic arena events that challenge players and keep matches exciting.
- **Power-Ups & Obstacles:** Developed a variety of power-ups and obstacles to enhance replayability and strategic depth.
- **Visual & Audio Feedback:** Integrated impactful visual and audio cues for player actions and game events.
