Hello! I’m Harry Stansfield, a Gameplay Designer & Programmer based in Manchester, UK. I’m passionate about designing and building engaging games, with a focus on creative mechanics, procedural systems, and polished user experiences.

## My Journey

I’ve developed award-winning games both independently and as part of teams, including:

- **Retrieve The Artefact** – Winner of the ACC Best Games Design and Development Award 2023
- **Shards of the Future** – Best Game at Staffordshire University Fresher Gamejam
- **MECHHEAD** and **DiscGO** – Developed during the Staffordshire Games Institute’s 1UP summer placement scheme
- **Echo** – University project graded 90

My portfolio showcases a range of projects, from procedural generation and custom shaders to advanced AI and dynamic UI systems. I enjoy tackling technical challenges and bringing ambitious ideas to life.

## Skills & Focus

- Gameplay programming (Unity, C#, procedural generation, AI)
- Game design and prototyping
- UI/UX for games
- Team collaboration and solo development

## More About Me

When I’m not working on games, I’m probably listening to relaxing music or traveling somewhere new. I love learning, experimenting, and pushing the boundaries of what games can do.
